    About Actify

    Actify is a small practice project for building daily habits through simple, action-driven challenges.

    The idea is that small, consistent steps add up. Each habit you add can be checked off once a day; doing it on consecutive days builds a streak, and missing a day resets it.

    There is no account and no server: habits and history are stored in your browser's localStorage, so they stay on this device until you delete them.
